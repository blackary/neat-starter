### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

As Jesus was being baptized by John, Luke tells us of the presence of the Father and the Holy Spirit and the announcement by God the Father to God the Son, *You are my beloved Son; with you I am well-pleased*. Here in Isaiah, God also declares His delight in His Servant/Son. The context (Isa 41:21-24) is God challenging the nations that their idols are false gods. Let your (false) gods demonstrate their sovereignty over past and future events and we’ll know you are real.

Behold, idols, you are nothing (Isa 41:24). Behold, these idols are make-believe (Isa 41:29). But, people of God, do you want something real? Behold my servant (v1). Jesus is real. Jesus is sovereign. Jesus is God’s servant. Jesus is the one that God the Father delights in. Jesus is a gentle servant (Mt 11:29) who brings God’s justice to the world through His salvation.

Idols won’t be discarded for good, injustice won’t be finally set right, sin won’t be forgiven, lives won’t be changed—apart from the salvation of God found only in Jesus Christ. God, who will not give His glory to another, His praise to carved idols, does give His glory to His Son. Therefore, God the Son is truly and fully God.

Not only does God love Jesus, God also loves all those He has given Jesus to save. By faith in Jesus, you know that you are also God’s servant, in whom He delights.
