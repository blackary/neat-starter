### Catechism

**WSC 88**

_What are the outward means whereby Christ communicateth to us the benefits of redemption?_

The outward and ordinary means whereby Christ communicateth to us the benefits of redemption, are his ordinances, especially the word, sacraments, and prayer; all which are made effectual to the elect for salvation.

<div><small><i>Matthew 28:19-20; Acts 2:42; Acts 2:46-47</i></small></div>

### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

The Westminster Larger Catechism instructs us about the use of the Lord’s Prayer:

*The Lord’s Prayer is not only for direction, as a pattern, according to which we are to make other prayers; but may also be used as a prayer, so that it be done with understanding, faith, reverence, and other graces necessary to the right performance of the duty of prayer. (WLC 187)*

Jesus, in His kindness, taught us how to pray. We can be sure that what He teaches is useful for us. After all, Jesus was a man of prayer. He spent much of His time on earth in prayer to His Father. And even now, as our Mediator, He makes continual intercession for us. So, learn from Jesus’s prayer how you also should pray.

For starters, you can look at questions 178-196 in the Larger Catechism and use that as you pray through the Lord’s Prayer. May God make all His people to be people of prayer!
