### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

People who follow Jesus will sometimes face trouble in this sinful world. We do not ask for persecution, and we may not want it, but it can happen. Even so, Jesus is in control of everything. When the early church was being persecuted, it did not stop growing. More and more people believed in Jesus. Soon there were about 5,000 men who were believers, plus many women and children.

The disciples could not stop talking about Jesus. Because of this, they were put in jail. The next day, Peter and John were brought before the Jewish leaders. The leaders wanted the disciples to stop telling people about Jesus. They ordered them never to speak or teach about Him again. Peter and John answered, "We cannot stop telling people about Jesus!"

A short time later, the disciples were arrested again. The Jewish leaders warned them once more not to speak about Jesus, and they even beat them. But when the disciples were released, they did not complain. Instead, they were joyful because they had suffered for Jesus's name (Acts 5:40-42).

Even after all of this, they kept teaching and telling others the good news about Jesus every day.
