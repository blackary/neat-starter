### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)

The angel Gabriel told both Zechariah and Mary, "Do not be afraid." Why was Zechariah afraid? Because an angel suddenly appeared in front of him! Most people would be scared if that happened. Zechariah was shocked and frightened.

Gabriel's message was meant to comfort him. "Do not be afraid." Those are special words that appear more than 50 times in the Bible. People often feel afraid, but God wants His people to trust Him instead of living in fear.

Mary was afraid too. Like Zechariah, she was surprised and troubled when Gabriel appeared to her. Both of them saw something supernatural, something we don’t usually see. Angels are God's servants to His people (Heb 1:14), but seeing one in person would be more than a little scary!

Ever since Adam and Eve sinned in the Garden of Eden, people have been afraid of God because of sin (Gen 3:10). But Gabriel came with good news. He told Mary, "You have found favor with God." God had chosen Mary for a very special purpose. She would give birth to Jesus who is the source of God's favor and blessing for everyone who trusts in Him.
