**WSC #31**

_What is effectual calling?_

Effectual calling is the work of God’s Spirit, whereby, convincing us of our sin and misery, enlightening our minds in the knowledge of Christ, and renewing our wills, he doth persuade and enable us to embrace Jesus Christ, freely offered to us in the gospel.

<div><small><i>(2 Timothy 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:13-14; Acts 2:37; Acts 26:18; Ezekiel 36:26-27; John 6:44-45; Philippians 2:13)</i></small></div>

### Notes

(See below for all authors.)	

The devil comes to Jesus while He is hungry from a 40-day fast. He tempts Him with bread, not forbidden fruit, saying that if Jesus is really God’s Son, he could make bread from stones.

Eating bread after a fast is not, by itself, sinful. But how can God’s own Son be hungry? Is God not providing for Him? But Jesus will have none of Satan’s deception. He knows the Bible and quotes it to Satan. God’s word—His creative, energizing, and sustaining power, not bread, is ultimately what man needs.

The devil takes Jesus to a high mountain. There, he shows Him the glory of all the kingdoms of this world. *All yours*, promises the devil, *if you’ll just worship me*. All for the price of one bow. What a bargain! No need for a painful death on a cross in order to be given all authority. Just a bow. But Jesus answers that God alone is the right object of worship.

Now the devil takes Jesus to the pinnacle of the temple in Jerusalem. *Jump*! the tempter tempts. *After all, God says in His Word that His angels will keep safe those who are His*. Again, Jesus says, “No.” Although Satan quotes the Bible accurately, he does not apply it correctly. To foolishly presume on God’s care in reckless action is to sinfully test God. Jesus did not give in. Ever.
