### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Luke tells us that the crowds were astonished (v32), amazed (v36), and marveled (v22) at Jesus’s teaching. I hope you are too.

Jesus taught with gracious words (v22) and with authoritative words (v32, v36).

He demonstrated authority over demons and sickness. This was some of the evidence of the year of the Lord’s favor that Jesus said was fulfilled in Him. The people saw His authority and were astonished and amazed. The religious leaders saw His authority and were threatened. *Who gave you this authority*, they would ask him later in His ministry (Lu 20:3). But He refused to tell them. Satan offered Jesus authority over all the kingdoms of the earth if Jesus would but worship him (Lu 4:5–6). But it wasn’t Satan’s to give.

So, where did Jesus get His authority? He would tell His disciples after He was raised from the dead that He was granted all authority by His Father (Matt 28:18). Even though He hadn’t yet been raised, His authority did come from His Father. As the Son of God, He was given authority to preach and to heal, and even to forgive sins (Mark 2:10).

What will you do with Jesus’s authority? Will you marvel and be amazed and astonished? Will you submit to His authority?
