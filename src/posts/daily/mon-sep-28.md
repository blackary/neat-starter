### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

God directed His people to set aside each seventh year as a Sabbath year. In this year they were to let the land rest from normal farming activities but were free to eat what the land produced on its own. God said that the land was His (v. 23).

Every seven cycles of Sabbath years were to be followed by a Year of Jubilee—a year of the Lord’s favor. Though Leviticus uses different language than Isaiah, this was to be a year of the Lord’s favor as Mark quotes from Isaiah 61:1-2. In this year, again the land would lie at rest, but they could eat what it produced on its own. Property that had been sold was to be returned to the original owner. Slaves were to be freed.

This year was a year of God’s favor to His people. It was a year to proclaim liberty throughout the land (v10). Though we no longer grow all our own crops, we still need the Lord’s favor. And we still need to proclaim the Lord’s favor and salvation. We read in another place in the New Testament, *we also appeal to you, “Don’t receive the grace of God in vain.” … now is the acceptable time; now is the day of salvation!* 2 Cor 6:1–2 (CSB). God’s favor is available in the salvation that Jesus purchased.
