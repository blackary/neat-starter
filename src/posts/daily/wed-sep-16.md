### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Jesus was a real boy. But a boy who never sinned. How did Jesus keep His ways pure? By guarding it the way God’s Word told Him to. He did that perfectly, every day of His life. He did that perfectly, every time He was tempted to sin.

Whether you are a young man, or a young woman, or an older man or an older woman, how can you keep your way pure? Exactly the same way Jesus did. That is, guard it the way God’s Word tells you to.

Psalm 119 is full of references to God’s Word. Yet it’s even more full of the psalmist speaking to God about his life and God’s Word_. I, me, my, You, and Your_ are the most common words. Consider Jesus, growing in strength and wisdom (Lu 2:40), increasing in wisdom and stature, finding God’s and man’s favor (Lu 2:52) singing, reading, praying these verses from Psalm 119.

_I have sought You with all my heart; I have treasured Your word in my heart; I rejoice in the way revealed by Your decrees as much as in all riches; I will … think about Your ways; I will not forget Your word_. And in Jesus, united to Him by faith, we can and must live and love this way, too.
