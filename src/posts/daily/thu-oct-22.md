### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Ezekiel was born during Josiah’s reign. Josiah was the last good king in Judah. In fact, he is one of three kings referenced as loving God. Do you know the other two? (David and Hezekiah)

Ezekiel was likely born within a year of the time Josiah found the book of the law and began reforms in Judah (see 2 Kings 22 and earlier in 23).

God came to Ezekiel and called him to be a prophet to the exiles in Babylon in the fifth year of the exile of Jehoiachin (Ezekiel 1:2).
