### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)

When I was growing up, the story of Jesus's birth from Luke 2:1-14 was printed in the newspaper every Christmas Day. It was also recited by Linus in the famous _A Charlie Brown Christmas_ TV special each year. Lots of people knew the story, but sadly, many people did not understand how important it really is.

The birth of Jesus is much more than a Christmas story. It is a life-changing story! Jesus the Savior was born. He is Christ the Lord, the One God sent to save His people from their sins. Because of this, we praise God whenever we hear or think about this story, not just at Christmas but all year long.

The story begins with an important ruler named Caesar. He ordered people throughout his empire to register their names. Because of this order, Mary and Joseph had to travel to Bethlehem, the hometown of Joseph's family. Even though Caesar made this decision, God was still in control. Long before Jesus was born, God had promised that the Savior would be born in Bethlehem. This trip was to make that promise come true.

The first people to hear the good news were not rich or famous. They were humble shepherds watching their sheep in the fields. A great crowd of angels appeared and praised God! The angels announced the wonderful news that a Savior had been born.

This was good news for everyone. Rulers need a Savior. Parents need a Savior. Shepherds need a Savior. And we need a Savior, too.
