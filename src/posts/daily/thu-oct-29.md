### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Jesus had compassion. We see that all throughout the gospel accounts. Jesus has compassion. We who are His have seen that in Jesus saving us and caring for us.

Do you ever care so much for someone who is hurting in some way that you actually get a stomachache? There is a sense of that in this word compassion. Luke tells us in v. 13 that Jesus had this compassion for the widow of Nain whose only son had died. The funeral procession crossed paths with the crowd travelling with Jesus.

Bentley notes that Jesus *was filled with compassion and said, ‘Don’t cry.’ This is what we too say in similar circumstances, but unlike us, Jesus had the power to do something which could bring joy into the sadness. He went up to the coffin and touched it*. Then he raised the dead son to life!

But Jesus’s compassion is not limited to this sad widow. His life on this earth was marked by compassion. Certainly Jesus showed compassion to the centurion whose servant He healed. And He shows compassion to us. Christ died for us—sinners (Rom 5:8).

Might we give God glory for the power and the compassion of our Savior.
