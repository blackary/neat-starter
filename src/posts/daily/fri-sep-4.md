### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

The Gospel of Luke begins by telling us about a priest named Zechariah. One day while he was serving in the temple, an angel named Gabriel appeared to him. Gabriel had come directly from God's presence. Not surprisingly, Zechariah was frightened. But Gabriel brought good news, even though it seemed impossible.

Zechariah and his wife were very old and had never been able to have children. Yet God promised that they would have a son. His name would be John, and he would prepare people for the coming of the Lord. Like the prophet Elijah, John would help turn people's hearts back to God and bring families together. At first, Zechariah didn’t believe the angel's message, so God made him unable to speak until the baby was born.

When John was born, Zechariah's speech returned, and he immediately began praising God. Everyone around him realized that this child was special. John was not the promised Messiah, but he would announce that the Messiah was coming.

Filled with joy, Zechariah praised God for His goodness. He celebrated God's kindness, His salvation, His faithfulness to His promises, His forgiveness of sins, and the peace He gives to His people.

Just as Zechariah shared this good news, we can share it too.
