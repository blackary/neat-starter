_Who is the Redeemer of God’s elect?_

The only Redeemer of God’s elect is the Lord Jesus Christ, who, being the eternal Son of God, became man, and so was, and continueth to be, God and man in two distinct natures, and one person, for ever.

_(1 Timothy 2:5-6; John 1:14; Galatians 4:4; Romans 9:5; Luke 1:35; Colossians 2:9; Hebrews 7:24-25)_

### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)

Luke carefully wrote down the story of Jesus's life and teachings. But he did not do it all by himself. God helped him through the Holy Spirit. The Bible says that God's people spoke His words as they were guided by the Holy Spirit (2 Peter 1:21).

Luke was not the only Bible writer who was helped by God. All the people who wrote the books of the Bible were guided by the Holy Spirit so that God's message would be true and clear. Because of this, we can learn about God, believe in Him, and be saved.

Some older Bible translations say that Scripture is "inspired by God." A better way to say it is that Scripture is "God-breathed." This means that the words of the Bible come from God. He used human writers, but the message is His.

Because the Bible comes from God, every part of it is useful. First, it teaches us what is true and what God wants us to believe and do. Second, it shows us when we are doing something wrong. Sometimes, when we read the Bible or hear it taught at church, we realize that we have sinned. We should be honest and say, "That's talking about me. I was wrong, and I'm sorry.” Third, the Bible helps us know how to change. It shows us how to repent, which means turning away from sin and turning back to God. Finally, the Bible trains us to live the way God wants us to live.

The whole Bible is God-breathed and useful.
